Part 01 · Our collection plan

Four data products

Our data comes in four linked parts: raw, processed, infrastructure, and event.

→ →
PRIMARY RECORD
Contents
Camera, LiDAR, GNSS/IMU, vehicle state, AV system diagnostics, and raw autonomy topics.
Format
ROS bag and its topic list. Images, point clouds, and timestamped CSV are extracted from the bag.
ANALYSIS-READY RECORD
Contents
Localization, trajectories, commands, vehicle states, environmental conditions, road conditions, and other annotations.
Format
Tables keyed on the camera image timestamp. Each row carries the corresponding LiDAR frame, vehicle state, driving commands, and world coordinates.
HD MAP RECORD
Contents
Road geometry, lane markings, surface condition, signs, intersections, shoulders, work zones, and visibility.
Format
HD map layers in Lanelet2 or GeoJSON, with route tables, geotagged images, and inspection records.
RESEARCH-FOCUSED RECORD
Contents
Events at three levels: degradation, warning, and disengagement.
Format
Clips of the raw and processed data around each event window, with the corresponding disengagement metrics.

Part 02 · Our research focus

Event Data

Not every problem ends in a disengagement. Grading by severity keeps the milder events in the record, so we can ask how often a degradation escalates into a warning, and a warning into a disengagement.

WORKING DEFINITION

An event is a departure from normal automated driving. We record events at three escalating levels: degradation, warning, and disengagement.

LEVEL 01Degradation

Performance or confidence declines while automated driving stays engaged.

→
LEVEL 02Warning

The system alerts the driver or requests a takeover, with automation still engaged.

→
LEVEL 03Disengagement

Automated driving ends, either by system fallback or by driver intervention.

Part 03 · Collection platform

The platform that produces the data

We develop on two AV stacks, DataSpeed and Autoware. Their sensor, autonomy, vehicle-interface, and system-state outputs are recorded and transformed into the four connected data products.
